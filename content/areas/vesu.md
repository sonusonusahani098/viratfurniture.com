Vesu is where Surat's new money settles down — the high-rise belt between VIP Road and Dumas Road fills a little more each year with families who've upgraded from older parts of the city and expect their homes to show it. Furniture briefs here read differently: walk-in wardrobes rather than two-door units, kitchens in acrylic and quartz conversations, panelled feature walls, and a finish standard where "almost flush" doesn't pass.

We've built for Vesu through its entire boom — individual flats, bungalows in the older lanes, and builder packages across the towers themselves.

## What premium actually means in execution

Vesu's expectations aren't about spending more; they're about noticing more. Shutter gaps even to the millimetre. Grain direction planned across a wardrobe face rather than left to chance. Handles aligned through a full kitchen run. Interiors — because in a walk-in wardrobe, the inside *is* the product — finished as carefully as the fronts. That's execution-level furniture-making, and it's where a 15-member in-house team with two decades of habit earns its keep. We bring the same construction standards everywhere in Surat; in Vesu, clients inspect for them with a torch, and we're comfortable being inspected.

## The Vesu order book

**Walk-in wardrobes and dressing rooms** — the area's signature request. Full-height systems with glass-shutter sections, island drawers where space allows, lit hanging runs and dedicated storage for the wedding-and-festival wardrobe a Surat family actually owns.

**[Modular kitchens](/services/modular-kitchen)** in premium formats — acrylic and PU finishes, tall units, built-in appliance planning, and islands in the larger tower layouts.

**Living rooms as compositions** — [TV walls](/services/tv-unit) with [panelling](/services/wall-panel), display for the collections, and bar or crockery units finished like showpieces.

**[Pooja units](/services/pooja-unit)** given proper real estate — Vesu flats increasingly plan a full pooja alcove, and we build both the traditional carved language and the modern backlit one.

**Bungalow work** in the older Vesu lanes — full-home custom projects where solid wood, veneer and longer timelines produce heirloom-grade pieces.

## Working inside the towers

High-rise work is its own logistics discipline: service-lift bookings, society work-hours, protection of common areas, and coordination with the parallel trades most Vesu flats run (POP, electrical, stone). Our modules arrive finished from the Palanpur workshop, which cuts on-site time dramatically — a full flat's furniture installs in days, not the weeks of an on-site build, and the society office stays friendly throughout.

For builders and designers working Vesu projects, we execute at package scale too — our [builder furniture work](/services/builder-furniture-work) runs sample-flat-first, then repeats the approved standard across floors.

## Designers welcome, drawings honoured

A good share of Vesu work arrives through interior designers and architects. We're comfortable as the execution partner — building precisely to issued drawings, flagging buildability issues early, and matching specified finishes — or as design-and-build where the family prefers one accountable team. Either way, the drawings get respected and the details get built as drawn.
