Adajan is one of the areas we work in most, and it's easy to see why — it's a mix of established housing societies, newer high-rise towers, and a steady stream of families upgrading or renovating homes near VIP Road and the Tapi riverfront. Whether it's a single wardrobe for a resale flat or furniture for an entire new tower, our team handles Adajan projects regularly enough to know the area's building layouts well.

## Furniture work we do in Adajan

Most of our Adajan projects fall into a few categories:

- **Wardrobes and bedroom sets** for both new flats and renovations of older homes, including space-saving sliding wardrobes for smaller bedrooms.
- **Modular kitchens** for families upgrading from an older kitchen layout to something more efficient, with better storage and easier maintenance.
- **Living room furniture** — TV units, storage cabinets and display units designed to fit the specific wall dimensions of Adajan apartments, which vary a fair bit between older and newer buildings.
- **Office furniture** for the commercial and mixed-use spaces along VIP Road and nearby, including workstations and reception counters for small offices and shops.
- **Full apartment fit-outs** for builders and owners furnishing flats before possession or resale.

Because Adajan has both older independent housing and newer high-rise developments, room sizes and layouts vary more than in a single planned society. That's exactly where custom measurement matters — a wardrobe design that works in one tower's 10x10 bedroom often won't fit cleanly in an older building's slightly different layout next door.

## How we work with Adajan homeowners

The process starts the same way for every Adajan project: one of our team visits your home, takes precise measurements, and talks through what you actually need the space to do. From there we put together a design and a clear, itemised quotation — plywood grade, hardware, finish and labour spelled out, not bundled into a vague total. Nothing goes into production until you've signed off on both the design and the price.

Manufacturing happens at our own workshop, not through a subcontractor, so the piece that arrives at your Adajan home matches what was designed and approved. Installation includes on-site adjustment for walls, flooring and skirting that are rarely perfectly uniform, especially in older buildings.

## Builders and societies

A meaningful share of our Adajan work is with builders and interior contractors fitting out multiple units in the same project — matching wardrobe and kitchen designs across several flats in a tower, or furnishing show flats ahead of a launch. We're set up to repeat a design accurately across units while still adjusting individually where room dimensions differ slightly, which matters more than people expect once you're past the second or third flat in a building.

For housing societies managing common renovations — replacing old wooden cupboards across several flats, for instance — we can coordinate timing across units so residents aren't all without furniture at the same time.

## Materials suited to the area

Adajan's proximity to the river means humidity is a real factor in material choice, particularly for kitchen and lower-storage units. We typically recommend BWP (boil-resistant) plywood for kitchen carcasses and any furniture near bathrooms, with MDF or standard plywood elsewhere depending on budget and use. Finish options — laminate, veneer or PU polish — are explained during the design stage so you're choosing based on how the piece will actually be used day to day, not just how it looks in a sample swatch.
