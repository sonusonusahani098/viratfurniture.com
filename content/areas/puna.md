Furniture here is a space negotiation, and we've been negotiating on the families' side for years.

## The compact-flat toolkit

Puna's 1 and 2BHK layouts reward every space-smart move we know:

- **Full-height [wardrobes](/services/wooden-wardrobe)** — wall-to-wall, floor-to-ceiling, loft on top; the whole wall working, no dust shelf left behind
- **[Sliding doors](/services/sliding-wardrobe)** in bedrooms where hinged shutters would clash with the bed frame daily — the area's most-thanked upgrade
- **Hydraulic storage beds** — the store room these flats never had, hiding under the mattress
- **Corner and alcove conversions** — [study surfaces](/services/study-table) and slim [storage](/services/storage-cabinets) built into the widths catalogue furniture can't use
- **Doorway lofts** throughout — suitcase and festival storage overhead, shutters finished to disappear

The free measurement visit in Puna is genuinely productive: most flats hide thirty-plus square feet of usable storage, and finding it is our favourite parlour trick.

## Growing families, returning clients

The Puna pattern: furniture keeps pace with the household. The flat furnished at possession gets its [study unit](/services/study-table) when school intensifies, bunk arrangements when siblings share, bigger book storage by board-exam years, and a kitchen reface when the original has cooked its ten thousand meals. Much of this work returns to us from families whose first furniture we built — finish codes on file mean every addition matches, and the trust compounds visit by visit.
