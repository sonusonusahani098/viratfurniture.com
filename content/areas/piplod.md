Piplod grew up before the towers arrived — its lanes off Dumas Road filled with well-built homes when this was the quiet, green end of the city, and the multiplexes and cafes followed. Today it's one of Surat's most settled premium pockets: families who've lived here for years, homes with good furniture that's aged honestly, and newer towers filling the gaps toward Gavier. The furniture work this mix generates is distinctive — less "furnish an empty shell," more "upgrade what's here, thoughtfully."

## The renovation-first neighbourhood

Established Piplod homes tend to hold furniture from an era when carpentry was solid — teak-framed sofas, sheesham dining sets, wardrobes built heavy. Twenty years on, the bones remain excellent while surfaces and hardware show their age. This is textbook [renovation](/services/furniture-renovation) territory, and it's most of what we do here:

- **Kitchens refaced** — carcasses kept, new shutters and soft-close hardware, often with counter upgrades via the family's stone vendor
- **Wardrobes modernised inside** — interiors reworked to current storage habits, hinges and channels replaced, faces re-laminated or [re-polished](/services/polishing)
- **Solid wood revived** — dining sets, beds and swings stripped and refinished until the original wood glows again
- **Sofas rebuilt** — frames kept, foam and fabric renewed through our [sofa repair](/services/sofa-repair) line

The economics are compelling — typically well under half of replacement — but Piplod families choose it as much for continuity: the home improves without losing itself.

## New work that respects old rooms

When Piplod homes do commission new furniture, it usually has to live alongside existing pieces — which makes tone-matching the first skill required. We sample against what you own before building: laminate and veneer selections held up in your room's light, polish tones tested on concealed patches, proportions drawn to sit comfortably with the furniture staying. The result reads as one home, not two eras arguing.

The most-commissioned new pieces here: [living room compositions](/services/living-room-furniture) — TV walls with panelling and display for collections a settled home has accumulated — [study setups](/services/study-table) as work-from-home became permanent, and [pooja units](/services/pooja-unit) upgraded from shelf arrangements to proper mandirs.

## Tower flats toward Gavier and Dumas Road

Piplod's newer edge — the towers rising toward Gavier Lake and along Dumas Road — brings the other kind of work: fresh fit-outs for possession-stage flats, with the full [apartment package](/services/apartment-furniture-installation) treatment. Same premium finish expectations as neighbouring Vesu, same workshop standards from us.

## Occupied-home manners

Much of Piplod lives in its homes while improving them, so our working style matters: manufacturing happens at our Palanpur workshop, site days are assembly days, rooms are phased so the family always has its spaces, and evenings end with the home tidy. Renovation without exile — it's a promise we keep weekly here.
