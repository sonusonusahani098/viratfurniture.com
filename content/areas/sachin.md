Sachin runs on production — the GIDC's lanes hold one of Gujarat's densest concentrations of working units, from textiles to engineering, and the township around them houses the families who keep the machines fed. Furniture here is infrastructure: the merchant cabin where orders close, the accounts floor where paperwork lives, the home where a working family recharges. We've supplied all three for years.

## Inside the estate

Factory furniture briefs are refreshingly direct — durable, functional, delivered without disrupting output — and our GIDC work answers in kind:

- **Admin and accounts floors** — [workstations](/services/office-workstation) with wiring discipline, storage walls for documentation-heavy trades, and desking that survives triple shifts
- **Merchant and director cabins** — the rooms where buyers sit; presence built to city-office standard, because orders are won across these tables
- **Sample rooms** — for the textile units especially: shelving and display engineered for fabric rolls and sample books, which outweigh any office file
- **Site and dispatch offices** — tough desks, counters and [storage](/services/storage-cabinets) specified for dust and load
- **Canteen and utility furniture** — benches, tables and lockers built for workforce-scale use

GST invoicing standard, installation planned around shifts — evenings, Sundays, maintenance windows. Furniture arrives as finished modules; production floors never host sawdust.

## The township's homes

Sachin's residential pockets house working families with clear-eyed furniture priorities, and our home practice serves them accordingly: [wardrobes](/services/wooden-wardrobe) with lofts using full wall heights, [kitchens](/services/modular-kitchen) built for daily cooking, storage-first packages with itemised phasing, and [repair sweeps](/services/furniture-repair) that keep good furniture working — the free doorstep inspection and honest verdict included.

## Businesses beyond the gates

The estate's edges trade — shops, eateries and services for the GIDC's daily population — and take our [commercial](/services/commercial-furniture) counters, seating and display work, installed after hours so shutters never stay down.
