Every business has an address; ours has been Gaurav Path Road long enough that the street feels like a colleague. The workshop at G109 has watched the corridor grow from Palanpur Jakatnaka's edge into one of west Surat's liveliest stretches — showrooms, cafes, schools and society after society filling in toward L.P. Savani and Pal. For the neighbours who've watched us back, this page is a formality: you already know where to find us.

## The walk-in workshop

Gaurav Path Road residents hold the one advantage no other area can match — you can simply come in. Handle the plywood grades we quote instead of trusting adjectives. Flip full laminate catalogues. Cycle a soft-close hinge next to a cheap one and feel the difference in three seconds. Watch mid-production pieces get edge-banded and judge the workmanship before it's yours. Furniture decisions made over real materials beat catalogue decisions every time, and our neighbours get them free with a five-minute walk.

Mon-Sat, 9 to 7; Sundays by appointment; chai available on request.

## Home-street service levels

Proximity is a service feature, and this street gets our maximum: same-day site visits when schedules align, deliveries that cost us nothing to make punctual, and after-service — the settling hinge, the monsoon-stiff drawer — handled as a stroll rather than a scheduling negotiation. Twenty years of that has furnished a healthy share of the corridor's homes with our [wardrobes](/services/wooden-wardrobe), [kitchens](/services/modular-kitchen), [TV walls](/services/tv-unit) and full [new-flat packages](/services/apartment-furniture-installation) for the societies rising along the stretch.

## Furnishing the street's businesses

The corridor's commercial face — showrooms, cafes, clinics, coaching floors — is both our neighbourhood and our client list. We build their [counters](/services/reception-counter), display systems, [cafe seating](/services/restaurant-furniture) and office fit-outs, installed evenings and closed days so trading never pauses. There's a particular accountability in furnishing businesses that can see your shutter from theirs; we've always liked that pressure.

## The neighbourhood testimonial

A workshop's home street is its harshest review platform — every job we've done here walks past the shop and waves, or would let us know otherwise. That the waves stay friendly after two decades is the reference we're proudest of. If you're on or near Gaurav Path Road, skip the phone tag: drop in with your floor plan, your problem cupboard, or your first-flat dreams, and let's talk over real samples. For doorstep visits all the same — call, WhatsApp or the form, and we're there fastest of all.
