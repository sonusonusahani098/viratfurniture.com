Rander was a port and a town when most of Surat was fields — its lanes remember centuries, its old homes carry woodwork from eras when carpentry was patient, and its families hold furniture that has outlived the shops that sold it. Working here is different from working in the tower corridors, and we like it that way: Rander is where our restoration skills earn their keep.

## The furniture that came before us

Old Rander homes hold treasure disguised as daily objects — teak cupboards with hand-cut joinery, jhoolas that have rocked three generations, brass-handled chests, dining tables of wood you can't easily buy anymore. Time dulls their finish and loosens their joints; it doesn't diminish their worth.

Our [restoration and polishing](/services/polishing) work returns these pieces properly: old finish stripped with care, joints re-glued and re-blocked the traditional way, damage patched in matching wood, then hand-finished — spirit polish where authenticity matters, modern PU where daily-use practicality wins. [Cupboard repair](/services/cupboard-repair) revives the locks, hinges and runners of pieces built before standard hardware existed. The result isn't "like new" — it's like itself, cared for.

If you've inherited furniture you're unsure about, have us look before any decision. The assessment is free, and our honest verdict includes "this is worth more restored than replaced" as often as Rander deserves — which is often.

## New furniture for old rooms

Modern needs arrive in heritage homes too — wardrobes, kitchens, study corners. The craft is building them to belong: proportions scaled to high ceilings and deep rooms, wood tones matched to existing woodwork, detailing that nods to the home's age without pastiche. Our [custom furniture](/services/custom-furniture) practice handles this regularly in Rander — a modern [kitchen](/services/modular-kitchen) that doesn't fight a hundred-year-old house is entirely achievable, and we've built the proof.

Old-quarter logistics are part of the service: narrow lanes, tight stairs and first-floor living all handled by modular pieces sized to the access and assembled in place.

## The modern side of Rander

Along Rander Road and toward Adajan, the area's newer buildings bring standard modern work — [wardrobes](/services/wooden-wardrobe), kitchens, [TV units](/services/tv-unit) and full-flat packages for possession-stage flats, with all the usual society coordination. The same team serves both Randers; the skills just flex to the building's century.

## Old neighbourhood, old-fashioned accountability

Rander works on trust built slowly — families here call the workshop their father used, and switching is a decision made carefully. We've spent twenty years becoming that workshop for a growing circle of Rander homes, one restored cupboard and one honest quote at a time. The free doorstep visit continues the tradition: call, WhatsApp or send the form, and we'll come see what your home needs — or what it already has, waiting under an old finish.
