Althan is where young Surat settles down — the corridor between VIP Road and the Bhimrad canal has filled with towers whose buyers skew young families: first proper flat, kids in school nearby, EMIs planned carefully, and a home that has to work hard from day one. The area mixes these new societies with the older tenament rows and Althan Gam's long-standing homes, which keeps our work here pleasantly varied.

## Furniture that respects a young family's math

Althan projects tend to be planned sensibly, and we quote to match. The itemised estimate isn't a formality here — it's the phasing tool families actually use:

**Phase one, the backbone:** bedroom [wardrobes](/services/wooden-wardrobe) with lofts, the [kitchen](/services/modular-kitchen), and doorway loft storage — the built-ins that benefit most from the empty-flat window and anchor the home's storage for a decade.

**Phase two, as life fills in:** the [TV unit](/services/tv-unit), a [study table](/services/study-table) when school gets serious, the [pooja unit](/services/pooja-unit), extra [storage](/services/storage-cabinets) as toys and belongings multiply.

Because finish codes stay on file, phase-two pieces match phase one exactly — the home grows without looking patched together.

## New towers, known drill

For possession-stage flats in Althan's societies, our [apartment package](/services/apartment-furniture-installation) process runs its standard course: design from the floor plan before keys, manufacturing in parallel with registration, installation within days of handover, society formalities (lift slots, gate passes, work hours) handled as routine. Two to four weeks from bare shell to move-in ready is the normal Althan experience with us.

## Tenaments and the older homes

Althan's tenament societies and Gam-side homes bring different work: ground-plus-one layouts with real staircases and awkward under-stair spaces (we love those — they convert into superb storage), renovations of kitchens and wardrobes that have served a family's full first act, and [repair](/services/furniture-repair) rounds that fix a decade of small complaints in one visit. Row-house walls are rarely tower-straight, which is precisely why made-to-measure fits where catalogue furniture gapes.

## Kids grow; furniture should keep up

A distinctly Althan pattern: the flat bought when the child was two needs re-thinking when they're nine. Bedrooms re-planned for study space, bunk arrangements where siblings share, wardrobes re-organised from baby-stuff depths to school-uniform heights. We do this adaptation work constantly here — often for the same families whose original furniture we built, which is the kind of repeat business that keeps a workshop honest.
