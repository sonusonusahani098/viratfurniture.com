## Value without the shortcuts

The first-home trap is furniture that looks affordable and costs double through early failure — sagging shelves, hinges dead in a year, laminate lifting by the second monsoon. Our approach keeps the price honest and the build honest at once:

- **Structure where it matters** — ISI-grade ply for carcasses and load-bearing spans; the invisible board is the last place to save
- **Branded hardware at daily-use points** — the wardrobe hinge cycled ten times a day earns quality; we name brands in the quote
- **Sensible finishes** — mid-range laminates today look excellent; the catalogue's costliest page is rarely the value page
- **Itemised everything** — each piece, material and hardware line priced separately, so choices are yours and visible

Furniture built this way at Dindoli budgets is our proof that "affordable" and "built properly" aren't opposites.
