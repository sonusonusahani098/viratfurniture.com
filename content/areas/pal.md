Pal has spent the last decade turning from a quiet stretch beyond the canal into one of west Surat's busiest residential addresses — tower after tower coming up around L.P. Savani Road and the Pal RTO stretch, with young families moving in as fast as possession letters arrive. For us the area is practically home ground: our workshop sits on Gaurav Path Road in Palanpur, minutes away, and Pal projects have filled our order book for years.

## The new-flat capital of our order book

Most of what we build for Pal follows the same happy event — a family getting keys to a new 2 or 3BHK. New towers hand over bare shells, and the weeks between possession and move-in are furniture's golden window. Our [apartment furniture packages](/services/apartment-furniture-installation) are built around exactly that: measurement from your floor plan or a pre-possession visit, designs and quotes settled before keys, manufacturing done in parallel with your registration formalities, and installation crews in the flat within days of handover.

The staples of a Pal package: bedroom [wardrobes](/services/wooden-wardrobe) built wall-to-wall with lofts, a [modular kitchen](/services/modular-kitchen) sized to the builder's counter and plumbing, loft storage over doorways, and a TV unit for the living room. Compact second bedrooms in the area's 2BHKs are where our [sliding wardrobes](/services/sliding-wardrobe) earn their keep — no door-swing fights with the bed.

## We know these towers' rules

Anyone who has done interior work in Pal's newer societies knows the drill — gate passes for workers, service-lift slots to book, working hours the society office enforces, and deposits for interior work. We handle that paperwork weekly across Pal and treat it as part of the job, not an inconvenience. Because our furniture arrives as workshop-finished modules, the society sees boxes moving upstairs rather than boards being cut in the parking — which keeps security, neighbours and the chairman all comfortable.

## Row houses, older societies and upgrades

Pal isn't only new towers. The row-house lanes around Pal Gam and the earlier societies off Canal Road bring us renovation work — kitchens refaced after fifteen years of service, wardrobes upgraded with modern internals and soft-close hardware, and the odd full-home refresh when a family buys a resale property. Our [renovation](/services/furniture-renovation) approach keeps sound carcasses and renews surfaces and hardware, which suits these solidly built older homes especially well.

## Why Pal families call the nearest workshop

Distance matters in furniture more than people expect. Being ten minutes away means our site visits happen promptly, installation crews arrive when promised, and if a hinge needs adjusting a month after handover, that visit is easy for us to make — and easy for you to insist on. Combine that with two decades of work in Surat and an in-house team of fifteen (no outsourced labour), and the case for calling us first is mostly geography plus track record.

We also serve Pal's commercial pockets — clinics, coaching classes, and shops along the main roads — with counters, [office furniture](/services/office-furniture) and storage built for daily public use.
