Nanpura is Surat's dignified middle — the old city's edge where established families, long-standing practices and buildings with real history share tree-lined streets. Homes here were furnished well the first time, decades ago; offices here have letterheads older than most suburbs. The furniture work this generates is particular: restoration over replacement, refinement over reinvention, and craftsmanship that meets the standard the neighbourhood was built to.

## Homes that improve without changing character

Nanpura households rarely brief "make it new" — they brief "make it right." Our work follows:

- **[Restoration and polishing](/services/polishing)** for the solid teak and sheesham pieces these homes hold — stripped, repaired and refinished until the original wood speaks again
- **[Renovation](/services/furniture-renovation)** of kitchens and wardrobes whose frames were built honestly and deserve new faces rather than skips — refacing, hardware upgrades, interior modernisation
- **New pieces matched to old rooms** — [living room compositions](/services/living-room-furniture), study corners and [pooja units](/services/pooja-unit) built in tones and proportions that sit naturally among existing furniture
- **[Repair sweeps](/services/furniture-repair)** that clear an established home's accumulated list in one respectful visit

Older buildings mean non-standard walls, high ceilings and the occasional beautiful quirk — made-to-measure was invented for exactly this, and we scribe to reality.

## The professional quarter

Nanpura works in chambers and clinics — advocates, doctors, CAs and consultancies whose offices carry decades of practice. We furnish them accordingly: [reception counters](/services/reception-counter) with the gravitas a practice's front room needs, cabins and consultation rooms in finishes that age well, conference tables for the serious meetings, and the deep [file storage](/services/storage-cabinets) that legal and financial work generates relentlessly. Installation runs evenings and Sundays; a practice's hours are its own.

## Working manners for a settled street

Occupied homes and running practices set the terms here, and our method suits them: manufacturing at the Palanpur workshop, short clean assembly days on site, room-by-room phasing, evenings left tidy. Nanpura notices manners — ours have been passing inspection here for twenty years.

## The quiet standard

This neighbourhood doesn't chase trends or advertise its taste; it simply expects things done properly and remembers who delivers. That's a standard we're comfortable being held to — itemised quotes, named materials, honest advice about what's worth restoring (in Nanpura, usually more than owners assume), and after-service that answers the phone. The free visit starts it: call, WhatsApp or the form, for homes and practices both.
