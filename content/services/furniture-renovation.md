Somewhere between "fix that hinge" and "throw it all out and start again" lives the smartest money in furniture: renovation. The premise is simple — in most Surat homes, the furniture's bones are fine. Carcasses are square, structures are sound. What's aged is everything you see and touch: laminates from two design eras ago, hardware from before soft-close existed, interiors planned for a different family's life. Renovation replaces those layers and keeps the rest.

We've renovated everything from single wardrobes to entire resale flats at our Palanpur workshop, and it remains the highest-satisfaction-per-rupee work we do.

## The renovation toolkit

**Refacing** — new shutters and drawer fronts on existing carcasses. The kitchen keeps its cabinets, counter and plumbing; the bedroom keeps its wardrobe shell; both look newly built. This is the flagship move, at well under half of rebuild cost.

**Re-laminating** — fresh laminate over sound surfaces where full shutter replacement isn't needed. Combined with new edge banding, tired furniture sheds a decade.

**Re-polishing** — for solid wood and veneer, the [full refinishing treatment](/services/polishing): stripped, repaired, refinished to a chosen tone and sheen.

**Hardware upgrades** — the feel of furniture is its hardware. Soft-close hinges, smooth channels, new handles and locks change the daily experience more than any surface.

**Interior rework** — wardrobes reorganised (more hanging, better shelves, drawer inserts), kitchens upgraded with pull-outs and organisers, storage retuned to how your family actually lives now.

**Repurposing** — the dresser that becomes a TV console, the old showcase reborn as a crockery unit, the cot converted for the next child. Good wood, new job.

## Where renovation shines in Surat

**Resale flats** are the classic case: you've bought a home wearing someone else's taste. Before the demolition crew wins the argument, have us assess — sound built-ins refaced to your palette usually beat rebuilding on both cost and time, and the mix-and-match (keep these, replace those) gets you a coherent home fastest.

**Kitchens** age at the shutters first. A reface — new fronts, new hardware, maybe new counters via your stone vendor — delivers a "new kitchen" reaction for a fraction of one, in under a week, with dinner cooked at home every night of the work.

**Rental and commercial properties** renovate on pure economics: landlords refresh between tenants, shops and offices between seasons — our [commercial](/services/commercial-furniture) clients renovate more than they rebuild.

**Inherited furniture** renovates for love rather than math, and we treat those pieces accordingly.

## Honest scoping, side-by-side numbers
