The living room carries more jobs than any other room in an Indian home — it hosts guests, absorbs the day's clutter, holds the TV everyone argues over, and in most Surat flats it's also the corridor to everywhere else. Furniture for this room has to be planned, because every unplanned piece costs walking space the room can't spare.

We've been building living room furniture for Surat homes for over two decades — mostly as coordinated sets: a TV wall here, storage and display there, panelling tying it together, each piece measured to the wall it lives on.

## Start with the wall that works hardest

In most halls, one wall does the heavy lifting — the TV wall. We design it as a single composition: the [TV unit](/services/tv-unit) itself, closed storage below for the clutter that otherwise colonises the sofa, display shelving for the things worth seeing, and often a panelled backdrop that gives the room its character. Cable routes, set-top ventilation and speaker positions get planned into the carcass rather than drilled in as afterthoughts.

From there the rest of the room falls into place. A crockery-and-display unit near the dining side. A slim console behind the sofa. A shoe cabinet that swallows the family's footwear at the entry instead of a sad rack beside the door. Where the home needs it, a compact pooja niche worked into the same composition — or a full [pooja unit](/services/pooja-unit) if space allows.

## Storage that hides, display that shows

The difference between a calm living room and a cluttered one is rarely the sofa — it's whether everyday objects have somewhere closed to live. Our living room storage follows a simple rule: closed shutters at the bottom for the remote controls, chargers, board games and newspapers; open or glass-fronted display up top for the brass, books and photographs. Proportioned right, one wall absorbs the whole room's chaos.

## Panelling and the backdrop question

Wall treatment has become half the living room brief. We build [wall panelling](/services/wall-panel) in laminate, veneer and louvre/slat styles — behind the TV, behind the sofa, or wrapping a beam that was always awkward. Done in wood tones with the same finish family as the furniture, panelling is what makes a hall photograph like a designed room rather than a collection of purchases.

## Materials chosen for a hard-working room

Living room furniture takes sun from the balcony door, tea spills, and the daily traffic of hands. Carcasses go in ISI-grade plywood; surfaces take laminates chosen for scratch resistance in homes with kids, or veneer-and-polish where a warmer look matters and care is realistic. Hardware — hinges, channels, flap stays for bar or console units — is branded and named in the quote. Everything is manufactured at our Palanpur workshop and installed by the same team.

## The process, briefly

A free site visit and measurement, a layout conversation about what the room must hold, then an itemised quote you can phase. Manufacturing happens off-site — your living room stays a living room until installation, which usually takes one to three days. Most full living room projects run 3-4 weeks from design approval.

## One hall or a tower of them

Builders and contractors bring us living room packages for entire residential projects — repeatable TV wall and storage designs across dozens of flats, plus show-flat halls built to sell the rest. Both fit how our workshop runs.
