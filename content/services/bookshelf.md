Some homes have a few books; some homes have a library pretending to be clutter — stacks on the side table, a row on the windowsill, cartons under the bed from the last move that never got unpacked. The books aren't the problem. The absence of a proper shelf is. And a proper bookshelf is a more demanding piece of carpentry than most furniture, because books are relentlessly heavy and shelves that sag announce it forever.

Our Surat workshop builds bookshelves across the range — single wall units, kids' book corners, and full floor-to-ceiling library walls with ladders on the wish list.

## Engineering first, because books are heavy

A metre of packed books weighs 25-30 kg — a six-shelf wall unit can quietly carry a quarter of a tonne. We build for that reality: shelf thickness matched to span, mid-supports where runs get long, back panels that brace the whole frame, and wall anchoring on tall units so nothing tips (with kids in the house, this is non-negotiable). It's the invisible work that keeps shelf lines straight in year ten.

## Formats for how you actually read

**The library wall.** Floor-to-ceiling, wall-to-wall — the format that turns a collection into a room's identity. We mix open shelving for the active books with glass-fronted sections that shield the good editions from dust and monsoon air, and closed base cabinets for the paperwork nobody displays.

**The reading corner.** A shelf column beside a good chair and a lamp — often built with a [study table](/services/study-table) into one composition.

**The divider bookshelf.** Double-sided, finished both faces, separating a study zone from the living area — shelving and a [partition](/services/wooden-partition) in one footprint.

**The kids' unit.** Front-facing racks at toddler height (covers visible — that's what makes them pick books up), sturdier shelves above to grow into, rounded edges throughout.

**Display-mix shelving.** For homes where books share space with brass, frames and travel souvenirs — varied compartment sizes so objects and books each get their scale, a staple of our [living room](/services/living-room-furniture) storage walls.

## Materials and the sag question

Carcasses and shelves in ISI-grade plywood — MDF has its uses, but long book spans aren't the place for it and we'll say so plainly. Finishes in laminate or veneer-and-polish to match the room; edge banding on every shelf front; adjustable shelf systems where your collection's heights vary (art books and paperbacks don't share a shelf politely). Glass shutters use toughened panels in wooden frames with branded hinges.

## Straightforward to commission

Measurement visit, a conversation about the collection (rough count, tall books, display objects, kids), design with an itemised quote, then manufacture at our Palanpur workshop and installation — about a week for single units, 10-14 days for library walls. If a wardrobe or study project is already underway, a matching bookshelf added to the batch shares finishes exactly.
