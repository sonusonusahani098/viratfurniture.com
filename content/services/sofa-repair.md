Every family sofa earns its wear honestly — a decade of movie nights, guests, kids treating armrests as gymnastics equipment, and ten thousand cups of tea held above it. Then one day the middle seat has surrendered, the fabric's gone shiny at the arms, and something creaks whenever anyone sits. The showroom answer is a new sofa. The better answer, most of the time, is the workshop's: keep the frame your sofa was built around, and renew everything that wears.

Sofa repair has been part of our Surat workshop for as long as we've built furniture — and it remains the most satisfying work we do, because the before-and-after happens in the same week.

## What actually wears out (and what doesn't)

A sofa is a hardwood or ply frame wearing consumables. The frame — if it was built decently — lasts generations. Everything else has a service life: **foam** compresses and stops recovering (the sag), **webbing and springs** stretch or snap (the sink), **fabric** tears, fades and pills (the look), and **joints** loosen (the creak). Repair replaces the consumables and keeps the frame — which is why a properly restored sofa isn't a compromise; it's the same furniture, renewed where furniture is supposed to renew.

Our checklist covers all of it: joints re-glued and blocked, springs and webbing re-strung, foam replaced with the density you choose (softer lounge feel or firmer back-friendly support — your call this time), and covers rebuilt in fabric you select from sample books we bring to your door.

## Reupholstery: the second first-impression

New fabric changes a sofa more than people expect — a dated maroon three-seater comes back in a contemporary weave looking like new furniture that happens to fit your hall perfectly (because it always did). We stock and source across the range: tough family-grade weaves that laugh at kids, stain-shy textures for the tea-prone, leatherette for wipe-clean practicality, and premium fabrics when the sofa is the room's showpiece. Piping, tufting and cushion styles can stay faithful to the original or update along the way.

Wooden show-frames — the polished arms and legs on traditional sofas — get their own renewal through our [polishing](/services/polishing) service in the same visit to the workshop.

Loose legs, a snapped batten, a joint that needs re-gluing — often done at your home in one visit. Foam, spring and full reupholstery work travels to our Palanpur workshop and returns in 4-7 days, transport handled by us. For full living room sets we stage the work in pieces so your hall never goes seatless. ## The honest verdict, included

Not every sofa deserves saving; cheap frames from the fast-furniture era sometimes don't justify the fabric they'd wear. When that's the case, we say so at the inspection — it costs you nothing and keeps our repair work's reputation intact. But if your sofa came from a time when frames were frames, odds are strongly in its favour.
