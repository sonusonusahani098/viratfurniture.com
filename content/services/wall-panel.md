Paint gives a wall colour; panelling gives it presence. It's the difference you notice in every well-designed hotel lobby and every living room that looks "done" rather than merely furnished — some part of the wall has texture, rhythm and warmth that flat paint can't produce. In homes across Surat, that part is increasingly wooden panelling, and much of it comes out of workshops like ours.

We design and install wall panelling as part of full interiors and as standalone projects — a single feature wall is a perfectly good reason to call us.

## Where one wall changes the whole room

The craft of panelling is choosing the right wall and stopping there. Our most common installs:

- **The TV wall.** Panelling frames the screen, hides cable routes, and turns the hall's focal wall into a composition — usually built together with the [TV unit](/services/tv-unit) itself.
- **The headboard wall.** In bedrooms, panelling behind and above the bed anchors the room; fabric-wrapped panels add softness and a little quiet.
- **The dining wall.** A slat or veneer field behind the dining table gives the eating area its own identity in open-plan halls.
- **Entry and passage walls.** A panelled strip with hooks, a mirror and a console turns dead passage into a proper foyer moment.
- **Office reception walls.** The wall behind the reception desk carries the brand; panelling with the logo mounted on it is a staple of our [office furniture](/services/office-furniture) projects.

## Styles, from louvre to leather-soft

**Louvre / slat panelling** — vertical wooden battens with shadow gaps — is the current favourite for good reason: it adds rhythm, works with LED strips beautifully, and suits both warm and modern palettes. **Flat panels** in laminate or natural veneer with V-grooves give a calmer, more classic field. **Fabric and leatherette panels** bring bedrooms softness and mild acoustic damping. And combinations — wood slats with a mirror band, veneer with stone-look laminate inserts — let a large wall carry more than one texture without chaos.

All of it is manufactured in our Palanpur workshop: battens machined consistent, edges banded, finishes applied under control rather than on your wall.

## The practical bones

Panelling attaches to a concealed wooden framework fixed at limited anchor points, which means three practical wins: wiring for sconces, LED profiles or a mounted TV runs invisibly behind; minor wall unevenness — common in older Surat buildings — disappears without replastering; and reinforced mounting points get built exactly where the TV or shelves will hang. Materials follow our usual honesty: treated ply framing, ply/MDF panel bases, finish layers named in the quote.

## Timeline and mess (little of either)

Because panels arrive finished, installation is quick and clean — most single feature walls go up in one to two days, with a project total of about a week from design approval. Renovating families appreciate this most: a hall transforms over a weekend without the home becoming a site.
