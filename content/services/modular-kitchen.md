The kitchen is the hardest-working room in any Surat home, and it takes punishment no showroom talks about — pressure-cooker steam twice a day, tadka oil in the air, turmeric-stained fingers on shutters, and three months of monsoon humidity every year. A modular kitchen that hasn't been built for this reality starts swelling and sagging by its second year. Ours are built for it from the board up.

Virat Furniture has manufactured kitchens in Surat for over two decades — for individual homes, renovations and builder projects alike — and every one of them is made in our own Palanpur workshop, not resold from a catalogue.

## Designed around how your family cooks

Before we draw anything, we ask about the cooking. Who cooks, and standing where? Heavy daily cooking or mostly quick meals? Where do the month's groceries land? Mixer-grinder daily or occasionally? The answers place everything:

- The **hob zone** gets masala pull-outs and utensil drawers within arm's reach
- The **sink run** takes the punishment, so it gets the most moisture-resistant construction and a bin solution that isn't an afterthought
- **Tall units** hold the pantry at eye level instead of in deep, dark base cabinets
- **Corner mechanisms** — carousels or magic corners — rescue the L-corner every kitchen wastes
- **Appliance garages** park the mixer and toaster off the counter but plugged in and ready

This is the difference between a kitchen that photographs well and one that cooks well. We aim for both.

## Materials: where we're strict

Kitchens are where material shortcuts show up fastest, so our defaults are conservative. Base and sink cabinets in moisture-resistant BWP/BWR-grade plywood. Wall cabinets in quality MR-grade ply. Shutters in laminate or acrylic over ply/MDF depending on the finish you choose. Edges machine-banded so steam finds no way in. Hardware — hinges, tandem boxes, channels, corner units — branded, soft-close, and specified by name in your quotation.

We'll happily explain where you can save (shutter finish, internal accessories can come later) and where you shouldn't (carcass grade, hinges you'll cycle fifty times a day).

## Working alongside your other vendors

A kitchen involves more trades than any other room — mason, electrician, plumber, counter fabricator, chimney installer. Our job is to make the cabinetry land smoothly among them. We measure after civil work, confirm plumbing and power points against the design, manufacture while the counter is templated, and install in coordination so the sequence never stalls. For renovations of lived-in homes we plan the changeover tightly, because a family can't lose its kitchen for a month — typical cabinet installation runs 2-3 days on site.
