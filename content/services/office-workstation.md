Multiply one desk by forty and every small decision becomes policy: two inches of wasted depth per seat is a lost cabin's worth of floor; one unplanned power point per desk is forty extension cords crawling across the office. Workstation projects are planning problems before they're furniture problems — and that's how we treat them.

Our workshop builds workstation systems for Surat offices of every size, from six-seat agencies to full commercial floors, manufactured in-house and installed to a schedule your business can live with.

## Layout first, always

Before materials or finishes, the plan: how many seats now, how many in two years, which teams sit together, where the light comes from, where the server and printer live. From that we lay out the floor —

- **Linear / bench runs** for maximum sensible density — shared spines, clean sightlines, easiest wiring
- **Clusters of four or six** where teams work talking to each other
- **Cubicles** with taller privacy screens for call-heavy floors and focus roles
- **Hybrid zones** — most real offices want a mix, and a plan drawing settles it before a single panel is cut

Seat spacing, aisle widths and chair pull-back get dimensioned honestly. A dense floor that still moves freely is the mark of a good plan; a cramped one costs morale every single day.

## Wiring is half the product

Every seat today carries a laptop, at least one screen, a phone charger and often a desk light — workstations live or die on cable discipline. Our runs carry **under-worktop raceways** for power and data along the spine, grommets at every seat, and flip-up access panels where hot-desking demands it. We coordinate directly with your electrician and LAN vendor so points terminate inside the raceway. The result: no extension-cord jungle, no chair wheels chewing cables, and an IT team that doesn't hate the furniture.

## Privacy, storage and the human details

Fabric-wrapped or laminate **privacy screens** between seats cut visual noise and pin up the things people pin up. **Pedestals** — mobile two-drawer-plus-file units — give each seat lockable storage; end-of-run cabinets take team files; and where paper is serious (accounts floors, CA offices), we add storage walls from our [storage cabinet](/services/storage-cabinets) practice. Worktops go in commercial-grade laminate over ISI ply with impact-banded edges — surfaces that take years of laptop drag and lunch spills without complaint.

## Installed around your working hours

Workstation runs are manufactured as modules at our Palanpur workshop, so on-site work is assembly — scheduled evenings, weekends or floor-by-floor for running offices. A typical 20-30 seat floor installs over a weekend once electrical points are ready. Phased growth is normal: we keep your design and material codes on record so next year's seats match this year's exactly.
