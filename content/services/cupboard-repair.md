A cupboard's problems arrive in instalments. First one door needs a lift to close. Then the key starts negotiating. A drawer develops moods. The monsoon adds a swollen edge that scrapes. None of it stops you using the cupboard — you just adapt, the way families do, until everyone has learned the secret handshake every door demands. It's remarkable how much daily friction people absorb from one piece of furniture.

All of it is fixable, usually in a visit or two. Cupboard and wardrobe repair is bread-and-butter work for our Surat workshop, and has been for twenty years.

## The usual suspects, and their fixes

**Dropped, misaligned doors.** Hinges fatigue and screw seats strip — the door sags, corners clash, closing needs technique. We re-seat fixings properly (not just longer screws in chewed holes), replace tired hinges — soft-close upgrades welcome — and realign until doors meet flush.

**Drawers that fight back.** Worn channels, bent tracks, or old wooden runners past their smooth days. Branded channel replacements sized to the load, or runner dressing for heritage pieces. A drawer should open with one finger; we don't leave until it does.

**Locks and lost keys.** Jammed or broken locks opened without scarring the shutter, then replaced with fresh locks and keys — upgraded grades where documents and valuables justify it.

**Monsoon damage.** Surat's annual humidity tax: swollen edges, lifting laminate, a door that scrapes every July. Edges dressed and sealed, damaged panels replaced and finish-matched, and exposed board edges sealed against the next season — treating the cause, not just this year's symptom.

**Structural tiredness.** Racking shells, sagging shelves, backs coming loose. Re-squared, re-braced, and shelf spans reinforced for the loads they actually carry.

## Beyond repair: the interior refit

Plenty of structurally sound cupboards store badly — one hanging rod, three deep shelves, and chaos. An interior refit keeps the shell and rebuilds the storage: shelves at the heights your clothes actually need, double hanging, drawer inserts, trays for jewellery and watches, and the loft reorganised so suitcases stop bullying the daily wear. It's a fraction of a new wardrobe's cost and transforms the daily experience — the same thinking we bring to [new wardrobes](/services/wooden-wardrobe), retrofitted.

For faces rather than function, shutters can be re-laminated or re-polished — effectively a facelift — through our [renovation](/services/furniture-renovation) and [polishing](/services/polishing) services.
