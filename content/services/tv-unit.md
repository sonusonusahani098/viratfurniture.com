Every evening, most families look at the same wall for a few hours. It's odd how often that wall is an afterthought — a TV on a stray table, a nest of wires below it, set-top box balanced on the router. A proper TV unit fixes the wall you look at most, and it's one of the most satisfying single upgrades a living room can get.

Our workshop has built TV units for Surat homes for over 20 years — from compact bedroom units to full-wall entertainment compositions — each one measured to the wall, the screen and the seating distance it has to serve.

## What a well-designed TV wall gets right

**Height, first.** The screen centre should sit at seated eye level — roughly 40-42 inches from the floor for typical sofas. Showroom units routinely miss this; custom ones shouldn't.

**Wiring, invisibly.** Power, HDMI, speaker cable and the internet line all get routed through channels built into the unit, aligned to the plug points your wall actually has. Devices sit in ventilated compartments — set-top boxes and consoles need airflow, and shutters with mesh or gaps keep them cool and out of sight.

**Storage in the right proportion.** Closed storage below handles the physical clutter of media life. Display shelving — open, glass or backlit — carries the objects that deserve the spotlight. The screen stays the focus; the wall around it stays calm.

**A backdrop that finishes the room.** Many of our TV units pair with a panelled background — laminate, veneer or slat-style [wall panelling](/services/wall-panel) — which frames the screen and hides the wall's imperfections in one move.

## Floating, floor-standing, or the full wall

The right format depends on your wall and your appetite for storage. Floating units keep floors clear and suit compact halls; floor-standing consoles carry more weight and storage; and full-wall compositions combine both with tall cabinets flanking the screen. During the site visit we check the wall's construction — solid brick happily carries a floating unit loaded with books, while some newer partition walls call for designs that put weight on the floor instead. This is exactly the kind of detail that separates made-to-measure from off-the-shelf.

## Materials and finish

Carcasses in ISI-grade plywood, shutters in laminate, acrylic or veneer to match the room's palette, and branded soft-close hardware throughout — the same standard as the rest of our [living room furniture](/services/living-room-furniture). Backlighting, where wanted, is planned with proper LED profiles rather than a strip stuck on later. Every material and brand appears in the itemised quote.

Installation includes mounting, levelling, cable routing and a final check with your devices actually connected.
