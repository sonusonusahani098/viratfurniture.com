Walk into a furniture showroom and the bedroom section looks convincing — until you try to fit those exact pieces into your actual bedroom. The bed crowds the wardrobe, the side tables don't fit their gaps, and the dressing unit ends up in the corridor. A bedroom works when its furniture is planned as one set, for one specific room. That's the work we do.

Virat Furniture has been building bedroom furniture in Surat for over 20 years — from single beds to complete master bedrooms, and from individual family homes to sample flats that builders use to sell entire towers.

## The whole room, thought through together

When we design a bedroom, the pieces negotiate with each other. The wardrobe takes the wall that gives it full height and loft space. The bed position respects the window and the fan. Side tables get sized to the actual gaps left beside the bed, not a hopeful 18 inches. If there's a dressing unit, it lands where morning light actually falls; if a study corner is needed, it tucks into the leftover width with the same finish as everything else.

The result reads calm rather than assembled — one palette of materials and handles across the room, no piece fighting another for clearance.

## Beds built for how you'll use them

The bed is the anchor, and we build every kind:

- **Platform beds** with clean lines and a solid, silent frame
- **Box storage beds** with side-opening compartments for bedding
- **Hydraulic lift-up beds** — the full base opens upward, giving you a store room's worth of space in a flat that doesn't have one
- **Upholstered headboards** in fabric or leatherette, with the panel work done in our workshop
- **Kids' beds** at safer heights, including bunk arrangements where two children share a room

Frames are built in quality plywood or solid wood depending on budget and span, with the load-bearing points reinforced — a bed should be the last piece of furniture in the house to develop a creak.

## Wardrobes, dressing units and the supporting cast

The wardrobe half of the project follows the same made-to-measure approach as our dedicated [wooden wardrobe](/services/wooden-wardrobe) and [sliding wardrobe](/services/sliding-wardrobe) work — floor-to-ceiling, planned internals, branded hardware. Dressing units get proper task lighting positions, drawer trays for daily jewellery, and full-length mirrors where the room lacks one. Side tables come wall-hung when floor space is tight. For families where the bedroom doubles as a workspace, we fold a [study table](/services/study-table) into the layout in the same finish so it belongs to the room instead of looking like an office refugee.

## Materials that survive bedrooms

Bedroom furniture lives close to people — it takes moisturiser-handed drawer pulls, monsoon humidity, and the occasional child using the bed as a trampoline. We build carcasses in ISI-grade plywood, use moisture-resistant grades where walls back onto bathrooms, and fit soft-close branded hardware throughout. Finishes run from hard-wearing laminates to veneer-and-polish for a warmer, natural look. Every material and brand is named in the quotation.

## How the project runs

Free site visit and measurement first. Then a layout with material options and an itemised quote — you'll know the cost of the full set and of each piece, so you can phase the work if you prefer (many families do the bed and wardrobe first, dressing unit later). Manufacturing happens at our Palanpur workshop, and our own team installs everything in a scheduled visit or two. A complete bedroom typically takes 3-4 weeks from approved design to done.
