Every business has one piece of furniture that works as hard as its best salesperson: the reception counter. It greets every client, sets the price expectation before a word is spoken, and appears in the background of every walk-in's memory of your office. It's remarkable how many businesses run this scene from a folding table — and how completely a proper front desk changes it.

We design and build reception counters at our Surat workshop for offices, clinics, salons, showrooms and hotels — each one shaped by who walks in and what happens at the counter.

## Two sides, two jobs

A reception counter is two pieces of furniture sharing one shell. The **visitor side** does the talking: a confident front in wood tones, brand-colour laminate or lacquered glass, often with slat detailing and a lit reveal that photographs well; a transaction ledge at 42-44 inches where papers change hands. The **staff side** does the working: a 29-30 inch desk surface with wiring for the PC, phone, printer and card machine routed invisibly; drawers and shelves planned for the actual paperwork of your front office; a lockable cash or valuables drawer where the business needs one.

Where the public expects it — clinics especially — we build an accessible lower counter section into the composition rather than bolting it on later.

## The backdrop is half the project

Behind every good reception is a wall doing quiet work. We build the backdrop as part of the scope: panelling in wood, laminate or slat styles from our [wall panelling](/services/wall-panel) practice, sized and lit to carry your logo — either surface-ready for your signage vendor or with routed, backlit branding integrated in the build. Counter and backdrop designed together is what makes the reception read as one designed moment rather than a desk parked in front of a wall.

## Shaped by your kind of front office

A CA firm's reception handles couriers and file exchanges; a clinic's handles patient records, a card machine and a queue; a salon's sells retail products off the counter; a builder's office receives buyers who are quietly judging the finish quality as a preview of the flats. We ask what actually happens at your counter for ten minutes and design for that — storage, ledge widths, seating for the receptionist through a full shift, and durability where footfall is high. Counters take more physical wear than any desk in the building; commercial-grade laminates, impact-banded edges and branded hardware are standard.

## Built off-site, installed overnight

Like all our commercial work, the counter and backdrop are manufactured at our Palanpur workshop while your business runs undisturbed. Installation takes one to two days and schedules happily after hours or over a weekend — many of our receptions have gone from delivery van to greeting customers between a Saturday close and a Monday open. Typical project time is 10-14 days from approved design; itemised quote and GST invoice standard.
