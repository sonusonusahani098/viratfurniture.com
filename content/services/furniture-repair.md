Furniture rarely fails dramatically. It accumulates small surrenders — the drawer that needs a particular jiggle, the wardrobe door that won't quite close, the chair everyone knows not to lean back on, the bed that announces every turn. Each one is minor; together they make a home feel tired. And because no single problem feels worth a carpenter's visit, the list just grows.

That's the gap our repair service fills: one visit, the whole list, an honest quote, and furniture that behaves again. It's been part of Virat Furniture's work for all of our twenty-plus years in Surat.

## The whole-house repair visit

Our most popular repair format isn't a single fix — it's the sweep. You walk our carpenter through the house pointing at everything that's been irritating you; we inspect, quote item by item on the spot, and fix what's fixable there and then. Typical list from a Surat 3BHK: two sagging hinges, one drawer channel, a loose bed joint, a wardrobe door that dropped, a chair joint, and a kitchen shutter that never aligned. Most of it dies in one afternoon.

What can't be fixed on site — machine work, finishing, structural rebuilds — travels to our Palanpur workshop and returns within days, moved both ways by us.

## What we fix, honestly grouped

**Structure:** loose and broken joints, cracked members, wobbling legs, bed frames and cots (our [bed repair](/services/bed-repair) speciality), table bases. **Movement:** hinges, drawer channels, sliding tracks, locks, handles — the hardware layer where most daily irritation lives, replaced with branded parts. **Surface:** chipped laminates, lifting edges, water-damage swelling, scratches and burns — patched, re-laminated or re-polished via our [polishing](/services/polishing) service. **Upholstery:** through our [sofa repair](/services/sofa-repair) line — foam, springs and fabric. **Doors and misc:** interior doors that scrape or sag, shoe racks, mandirs, kids' furniture — the whole domestic zoo.

## Repair versus replace — the honest math

Good furniture deserves repair; bad furniture sometimes doesn't. The dividing line is usually the carcass: solid wood and quality ply frames from a decade or two ago are often better material than new budget furniture, and renewing their hardware and surfaces is money well spent. Flat-pack particleboard that's failed structurally usually isn't worth its repair bill, and we'll say so at the doorstep instead of after. The free inspection exists precisely to make that call with you — real numbers, no push either way.

## For homes, offices and everything commercial

Half our repair diary is commercial: offices with a floor's worth of workstation niggles, shops with counters that take daily abuse, hotels and restaurants where a broken chair costs covers, societies with clubhouse and common-area furniture.
