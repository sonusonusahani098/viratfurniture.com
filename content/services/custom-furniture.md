If you've spent any time flat-hunting or renovating in Surat, you already know the problem — most furniture sold in showrooms is built for an average room that doesn't exist. A wardrobe that's a few inches too deep for your bedroom wall. A TV unit that ignores the electrical point you actually have. A bed that leaves an awkward gap next to the window. Custom furniture solves this the direct way: we measure your space first and build the furniture to fit it, not the other way around.

At Virat Furniture, custom furniture is where most of our work starts. We've been designing and manufacturing furniture for Surat homes and offices for over 20 years, and in that time we've built for practically every kind of layout the city has to offer — compact 1BHKs in Varachha, wide villa bedrooms in Vesu, show flats for builders, and everything in between.

## What "custom" actually means here

Custom furniture isn't a fixed catalogue with a few size options. It starts with your room. Our team visits your site, takes exact measurements, and notes the details that off-the-shelf furniture always misses — a column that juts into the room, a low beam, a window that's off-centre, or a plug point in a spot no standard design accounts for. From there we sketch a layout that works with those constraints instead of around them.

This applies whether you need a single piece — say, a TV unit sized for the exact wall you have — or a complete room. For full rooms, we design wardrobes, beds, side tables and storage as one connected set, so the proportions, finish and hardware match throughout rather than looking like separate purchases pushed into the same room.

## Our process, start to finish

**1. Site visit and measurement.** Someone from our team comes to your home or site, measures the space, and discusses what you need it to do — storage, seating, display, or a mix.

**2. Design and material selection.** We put together a layout and talk you through material options — plywood grade, MDF, veneer or solid wood — along with finish choices like laminate or PU polish. Pricing differences are explained upfront so there are no surprises later.

**3. Quotation and approval.** You get a clear, itemised quote. Nothing starts on the shop floor until you've approved the design and cost.

**4. Manufacturing.** Your furniture is built at our workshop by our own carpenters — not outsourced to a third party. This is also where quality control happens, piece by piece.

**5. Delivery and installation.** We deliver and install on-site, including any final adjustments needed for a snug fit against your walls and flooring.

Most single-piece orders are ready in 7-10 days from design approval. A full bedroom or living room set typically takes 3-4 weeks, depending on complexity.

A lot of custom furniture work in this city isn't just about looks — it's about making the most of space that's tighter than people would like. We regularly design:

- Wall-to-wall wardrobes that use every inch up to the ceiling, including a top loft for out-of-season storage.
- Bed units with integrated drawer storage underneath, useful in smaller bedrooms without a separate storage room.
- Study and work-from-home corners built into unused wall space or alcoves.
- Modular living room units that combine TV storage, display shelving and cabinet storage in one piece.
- Furniture for entire towers — we work directly with builders and interior contractors on 12, 15, 18 and 21-floor residential projects, where the same design needs to be repeated accurately across dozens of units.

Because we manufacture in-house with a 15-member team, we can hold the same quality standard whether it's one wardrobe for a single flat or matching furniture across an entire floor.

## Materials and finish

We work with plywood (including BWP/marine-grade where moisture resistance matters, such as kitchen and bathroom-adjacent units), MDF for smoother, paint-friendly surfaces, and solid wood for furniture where strength and longevity matter most, like dining tables and bed frames. Finish options include laminate for durability and easy maintenance, veneer for a natural wood look, and PU polish for a premium, smooth finish on solid wood pieces. We'll recommend what suits your budget and how the piece will actually be used, rather than upselling the most expensive option by default.
